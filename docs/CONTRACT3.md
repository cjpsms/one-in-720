## 1. What cj wants (round 24, 2026-11-11)
- **Saturday lessons**: on a Saturday in the dorm, about 08:30-12:00, taught like core subjects / พก., but the student picks a group A, B, C or D and each group studies 4 periods. Saturday lessons are SEPARATE FOR EACH GRADE (ม.1 to ม.6 each have their own groups and subjects).

- **งานราตรี (night show)**: celebrates the students about to graduate; like a Chinese banquet but at night; kids perform; jobs: video editing, filming, sound recording, and mainly performing; 2-3 people are directors; one show per grade ม.1-ม.6.

- **งานรับน้อง (freshers' welcome)**: 3 days, in the hall too.
- **HOW**: these must be real mini games, not text: working a food stall at the fair, buying things, the contests; in the hall you sit and watch the screen; in class there are science experiments, and club crafts such as stringing a bracelet.

## 3. Calendar (E3 reads it from C2's `SCHEDULE`; all numbers PLACEHOLDER)
A game day is `calendar(day)` (util.js): cycle = 14 days (Mon-Fri, Sat+Sun in the dorm = dayInCycle 6 and 7, Mon-Fri, then home dayInCycle 13-14), 9 cycles a term, 2 terms a year.
- **Saturday study/club**: every dorm Saturday (dayInCycle 6). Blocks `sat1..sat4` replace the weekend `morning` block (08:30-12:00, 4 periods of 50 min with 10 min breaks); then lunch and the usual afternoon/evening. The choice is made at `termStart` (payload field `satGroup`: `'A'|'B'|'C'|'D'|'club'`, `keep:true` keeps last term's; default in routine mode: keep, else `club` for study 'relax', else the group matching the grade's weakest subject or A). Each grade has its own groups (C2 `SATURDAY[grade]`). Skipped/club Saturday = chill (small stress relief, peer, club bonus). NPCs choose a group too (cheap, rule-based).
- **Freshers (รับน้อง)**: days 1-3 of year 1 and of year 4 (new entrants and outsiders mix). Normal classes are replaced by hall sessions and team games (3 blocks a day), run as a sequence of mini games/hall watches (about 3-5 plays in total over the 3 days, the rest auto blocks). Gains peer, stress relief, some teacher trust.
- **Hall assembly (หอประชุม)**: `kinds`: `award` (the term-report Friday of each term; every student whose term GPA is 4.00 attends; the 4.00 player goes up on stage), `lecture` (1-2 per term, a weekday afternoon replacing p7-p8, random cycle), `special` (2 per year, e.g. a national day talk), `farewell` (a teacher resigns or turns 60: random about once a year; the teacher is replaced by a new fictional teacher with a new name in the timetable; students close to the old teacher feel it). Attending = mini game `watch`. Skipping is offered only for lecture/special: skip -> `skipRoll` (50% caught: made to attend anyway AND teacherTrust drops, else the free time is spent in the dorm/canteen and diligence is not lost). Award and farewell and freshers sessions cannot be skipped. A random hall item "trust gain" can fire after any attended session (teachers noticed you).
- **Loy Krathong fair**: term 1, cycleInTerm 6, the dorm Saturday (dayInCycle 6): blocks `fair_morning` (stall prep, normal Saturday classes are cancelled that day), `fair_afternoon` (choose where to work / own stall / wander and buy), `fair_evening` (stalls open, contests, float). 15-25 stalls (`makeStalls`), each dorm opens one, teachers open some at random.
- **Night show (งานราตรี)**: term 2, cycleInTerm 8, the dorm Saturday evening block `show`; role pick at cycleInTerm 3 (a `scene` pending), rehearsals are auto blocks on the Saturday afternoons of cycleInTerm 4-7 that add `pl.showPrep` (more prep -> better score), the night itself is the role mini game. Roles: act, camera, sound, edit, director (2-3 directors per grade, chosen among those who apply by trust/prep), none (watch the show). Everyone also watches the other grades' show items via a `watch` (auto) block.
- Existing Tuesday activities, club, elective stay as they are. A club or elective period may be a mini game too (section 4 triggers `science_lab`/`club_craft`).
- Random events (events.js) keep their rate: still at most one per day and none on a day with another stop.

## 4. Pending kind `minigame` (NEW, the 7th kind)
```
{ id, kind:'minigame', tag, game:'watch'|'serve'|'craft'|'pattern'|'dash'|'timing'|'rhythm'|'track'|'sequence'|'steps',
  title, text, clock, seed:int, difficulty:0..1, durationSec:number, params:{...per game, see 5},
  canSkip:bool, skipLabel?:string,          // hall lecture/special: "แอบหนีไปนอน" (the 50% rule), others: none
  context:{ eventId, stage, who? },          // eventId e.g. 'loy_stall', 'fresher_hall'
  placeKind:'hall'|'fair'|'stage'|'lab'|'activity'|'classroom' }
payload  { score:0..1, stats?:{...}, skip?:true, auto?:true }
```
- Engine: `act(payload)` clamps score, calls C2's `resolve(...)` for effects and log, returns the next pending as usual. `skip:true` is allowed only when `canSkip`. `defaultPayload()` returns `{ auto:true, score: expectedScore(stats, difficulty) }` (so `fastForward` and tests never block). The pending is saved with the game state and restored after load with the same `seed`.
- `createGame({ seed, mode, miniGames:'play'|'auto' })`, `game.setMiniGames(mode)`, `view().miniGames`. In `'auto'` the engine resolves every mini game itself with `expectedScore` plus noise and logs `เล่นอัตโนมัติ`; nothing opens. Default `'play'`. Balance tests run in `'auto'`.
- `difficulty` comes from the relevant stats (knowledge, energy, stress, peer, role skill) so the game is a bit easier when the student is in good shape.
- Also `scene` pendings (choices) are used between plays: which stall to work in, own stall menu and investment, role pick, contest entry, the farewell moment.
- Mini game results feed the day timeline: the block's `detail` shows the outcome line (e.g. `ขายได้ 14 จาน ได้ค่าแรง 60 บาท`), `tags` get `minigame`.
- `view().day.blocks[].placeKind` gets new values: `hall`, `fair`, `stage`. `block.what` is Thai (`งานลอยกระทง: ช่วยร้านครูเอก`).

## 5. The ten mini games (UI contract, U4 builds watch/serve/craft/pattern/dash, U5 builds timing/rhythm/track/sequence/steps)
Common module shape, file `src/ui/minigames/<game>.js`:
```js
export function play(container, spec, done) // spec = the pending; draw into container; call done({ score, stats }) once, when the game ends (or the player taps the always-visible "ให้ระบบเล่นให้" -> done({ auto:true, score:<bot score> }));
  // returns { destroy() } which removes listeners/timers.
export function bot(spec, rng)              // pure: a plausible score 0..1 for an average player (used for auto-play and tests)
export const INFO = { id, title, how }      // how = one Thai sentence of instructions
```
Rules for every game: DOM or canvas, no libraries; touch and mouse and the keyboard (arrows/space/enter where it makes sense); works at 375 px width; uses the css variables of the dark theme (`--bg --card --fg --muted --accent --good --bad`) and the serif/mono look of the 1in8b UI; honours `prefers-reduced-motion` (slower or step-based but still playable); deterministic content from `spec.seed` via `Rng` (`src/rng.js`); length about `spec.durationSec` (15-40 s); never block the page; show a clear start button ("เริ่ม") and a result line (grade tier word + score percent) before calling `done`; Thai text, no emoji (draw icons with CSS/canvas).
| game | used for | what the player does | `params` | score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `watch` | hall lecture, award, farewell, freshers hall, special day | a projector screen with slides; the eyelids droop (a sleepiness bar rises); tap/hold to keep eyes open (or tap the highlighted keyword when it flashes) and catch the 3 questions asked at the end | `{ slides:[{title,line}], keywords:[...], questions:[{q, choices[3], answer}], topic }` | stayed awake share and questions right |
| `serve` | Loy Krathong stall (work for someone, or own stall) | customers arrive with orders; tap the right ingredient/steps in order and hand over before the patience bar ends; own stall: more customers | `{ stallName, menu:[{id,name,steps:[ingredient ids], price}], ingredients:[{id,name}], customers:n, patience:seconds }` | served share weighted by speed |
| `craft` | creative / beautiful krathong, beautiful costume | pick and place parts (banana-leaf folds, flowers, candle, ribbon; or garment parts) on a base; scored on a hidden rubric (theme palette match, symmetry, variety, no overlap) | `{ kind:'krathong'|'costume', theme:{name,palette:[...]}, pieces:[{id,name,color,kind}], slots:n, rubric:{ palette, symmetry, variety } }` | rubric score |
| `pattern` | club craft (bracelet), souvenir | memorise a bead/colour sequence, then rebuild it by tapping beads in order, longer each round | `{ beads:[colors], rounds:n, startLen:n, label:'ร้อยกำไลข้อมือ' }` | rounds cleared share |
| `dash` | fast dressing contest, stall set-up race | tap garments/items in the order shown as fast as possible, mistakes cost time | `{ items:[{id,name}], sequenceLen:n, timeLimit:s }` | speed and accuracy |
| `timing` | float the krathong (release at the right wind/water moment), also titration | a marker sweeps, stop it in the green zone (3 tries, narrower each time) | `{ tries:3, zone:width 0..1, speed, label }` | closeness over tries |
| `rhythm` | night show: act/perform | notes (lines/beats) fall in 3-4 lanes, tap in time | `{ lanes:3|4, bpm, notes:n, label:'การแสดงบนเวที' }` | hit accuracy |
| `track` | night show: camera (keep subject in frame) and sound (keep the needle in the band) | hold/drag to keep a moving target inside the box/band for the duration | `{ mode:'camera'|'sound', speed, band:width 0..1 }` | time-in-band share |
| `sequence` | night show: video editing; stall menu order | drag/tap clips into the right story order (hints from the title cards) | `{ clips:[{id,label,order}], label }` | correct positions share |
| `steps` | science experiment in lab, class demo | follow a lab procedure: choose the next correct step among 3, plus one timed pour/heat hold | `{ title, steps:[{ prompt, choices:[3], answer, hold? }] , hazard }` | steps right and hold accuracy |
`stats` is optional flavour (e.g. `{ served, missed }`) and is only logged.
