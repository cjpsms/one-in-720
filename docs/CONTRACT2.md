# CONTRACT 2: routine mode, short play, and the "1 in 8 Billion" look

Read `docs/CONTRACT.md` and `DESIGN.md` first; this file ADDS to them (CONTRACT.md stays valid; where this file differs, this file wins). cj's words (round 20): "make the UI exactly like 1 in 8 Billion, adjust the balance too, the game should not take that long to play". Reference site: https://1in8b.life/en/ (open it in the built-in browser to look: landing, "Be born" birthplace roll, Birth registration card, life screen).

## 1. Goal
A whole 6-year life should take about 6 to 10 minutes at normal speed (1 school year about 60 s at 1x, like "1 year = 45 s" there), with a few real decisions per year, not 9,000 clicks. The player sets a **routine** (the "focus" of the term) and the school runs by itself; the game stops only for things that matter. The detailed per-meal / per-period screens (shop, classPlan, plan, lineOrder from CONTRACT.md) stay available as "detailed mode" for players who want full control.

## 2. Engine additions (owner E2: `src/engine/*.js`, `test/engine.test.js`, `test/balance.test.js`)
- `createGame({ seed, mode })`, mode `'routine'` (default) or `'detailed'` (old behaviour: every meal, block and evening is a pending). Saves keep the mode.
- Routine object (validated, stored in state, shown in view): `{ study:'focus'|'normal'|'relax', food:'cheap'|'normal'|'treat', evening:'study'|'balanced'|'social'|'rest', snack:'none'|'sometimes'|'often' }` (snack = LINE-shop snacking in class, with its 10% risk). Meaning, with PLACEHOLDER numbers in config: study = class stance and homework habit; food = meal budget per day (about 90 / 135 / 190 baht) and what is bought; evening = the 19:00-00:00 plan template (and bed time discipline); snack = how often a LINE order is placed and eaten in 221/223 classes.
- In routine mode every day runs automatically from the routine: meals, class stance, free time, required items bought when missing, parents asked when money is low, home trips auto-resolved (rest at home) with a log line, weekends auto. A pending appears ONLY for: `focus` (new kind, below, at the start of every term and when a term goes badly), `termStart` (club + elective), random events and teacher-snap scenes that involve the player, caught-in-class, a money emergency, the year report, the ม.4 gate, endings. Target: about 6 to 12 pendings per school year, about 60 per life.
- New API: `game.setRoutine(partial)`; `game.advance({ days = 1, untilDecision = true })` runs the school forward by up to N days or until a pending needs the player, returns `{ days, log:[{t,text,tone,day,yearLabel}], pending, progress }`; `game.view()` adds `routine`, `progress:{ yearIndex:0..5, termIndex:0..1, dayInYear, daysInYear, fraction }`, `label:'ม.1 เทอม 1'`, `stats4:{ health, happy, know, bonds }` (0-100; happy from stress/mood/sleep, know from knowledge, bonds from peer + teacher trust average), `record:[…last 200 log lines with day and yearLabel…]`, `birth:{ regNo:'720-2569-000123', sentence:'…born as…' }` (a registration number and a Thai sentence for the birth card), `schoolMap:{ grades:[{ grade, rooms:[count...] }], meRoom }` (counts for the dot picture). `fastForward` and `defaultPayload` keep working.
- New pending kind `focus`: `{ kind:'focus', title, text, fields:[{ key:'study'|'food'|'evening'|'snack', label, choices:[{ value, label, sub }] }], current:{...routine}, hint?:string (what went well/badly last term) }`, payload `{ routine:{...} }`.
- Balance (E2's second job, measured with a Monte Carlo in `test/balance.test.js`, 200 lives per routine, print a table): targets (cj wants meaningful choices, PLACEHOLDER targets): study 'focus' passes the ม.4 gate about 95% and graduates about 90%; 'normal' about 80% / 75%; 'relax' about 45% / 40%; average GPAX ordered focus > normal > relax with a clear gap; money never forces quitting unless food 'treat' + snack 'often'; teacher snaps involving the player about 3 to 8 a year; random events about 4 to 8 a year (not more); LINE-shop catches follow the 10% rule; sleep/health matter (evening 'rest' helps health, 'study' helps knowledge but costs health and happiness); roommates / bonds matter a little for happiness. Tune the numbers in `src/engine/config.js`, keep cj's rules untouched (rooms by GPA, gate, money rules, dorm times, shop prices).
- Every test from before must still pass (update tests where the default flow changed on purpose).

## 5. Round 22: watch the days (cj: "too fast, like a quiz game; add pauses, show what is done each day")
cj's feedback: the auto-play flies through weeks and only stops to ask questions, like a Q&A game. cj wants to WATCH each day: wake, breakfast, each class, lunch, afternoon, dinner, evening, sleep, with rest breaks. The Q&A stops must become rarer and feel like part of the story.

### 5.1 Engine (owner E2)
- Block-level stepping. `game.stepBlock()` advances the whole school by exactly one block of the player's day and returns `{ ok, done, block, log, pending, dayEnded, weekEnded, termEnded, yearEnded }`. A school-day block is one of: wake, breakfast, each period or activity block of the timetable (periods 1-8, tuesday activities, พก.), lunch, after-school free time (15:30-18:00, may be split in two), dinner, dorm check-in + assembly, evening (19:00-00:00, may be split in two), sleep. A weekend / home-trip day has: morning, lunch, afternoon, evening, sleep. `block = { id, from:'08:30', to:'09:20', minute, place:'ห้อง 218', placeKind:'classroom'|'lab'|'computer'|'canteen'|'coop'|'dorm'|'dormStudy'|'field'|'gym'|'activity'|'home'|'walk', what:'คณิตศาสตร์ กับครูมินท์', detail:'ตั้งใจเรียน ครูอารมณ์ดี', tone:'good'|'bad'|'neutral', spend:int, fx:{...}, tags:['lineShop','teacherSnap',...] }`. `advance({days})` and `fastForward` must be built on stepBlock and stay deterministic and equivalent (same state after N blocks however you step).
- `view().day = { label:'วันจันทร์ที่ 12', dow, schoolDay, blocks:[{ from, to, place, placeKind, what, detail?, done:boolean, current:boolean }] }` the day's timeline: done blocks carry their real `detail`, upcoming blocks carry only the plan (`what`, `place`).
- Recaps: `view().recap` is filled when `weekEnded` / `termEnded` / `yearEnded` is true (otherwise null): `{ kind:'week'|'term'|'year', label:'สัปดาห์ที่ 3 · ม.1 เทอม 1', rows:[[label,value]...], highlights:[string x3-6], money:{ spent, left, perDay }, sleepAvg, classesAttended, snacks, mood:'up'|'flat'|'down', rank?, gpa?, roomNext? }` built from the days since the last recap (term and year recaps summarise the term / year; year recap includes rank, GPA, next room, next dorm).
- Fewer, better stops: at most one random event per day and none on the day of another stop; about 6 to 10 stops per school year in total (term start picks count); `termStart` payload gets `keep:true` meaning "same club and elective as last term" and the pending carries `previous:{ clubId, electiveId }` and `canKeep`; focus card at term start only (plus the existing mid-term check only when things go badly). Events appear in stepBlock as a pending right after the block they belong to, with text that continues the day's story.
- Keep every earlier API, the detailed mode and all tests; add tests for stepBlock equivalence and for the recaps; keep the balance targets (re-run test/balance.test.js with BALANCE_N=60 to show nothing broke).

### 5.2 UI (owner U3)
- Life screen shows the day as it unfolds: a "วันนี้" timeline card listing `view().day.blocks` (time, place, what; done = solid with its detail, current = highlighted, upcoming = dim); the pixel scene follows `block.placeKind` and the real clock minute (night in the dorm, classroom by day, canteen at meals, field for PE...), the header clock moves block by block, the stat bars animate to their new values and a small floating "+/-" on changes, spending shows as a line like "ซื้อข้าวมันไก่ 35 บาท".
- Speed: stepBlock on a timer. 1x = one school day in about 10 s (about 0.7 s per block, a little longer on blocks with a log line), 2x about 5 s, 4x about 2.5 s per day, 8x about 1.2 s per day, "ข้ามวัน" (skip to end of day) and "ข้ามสัปดาห์" (skip to the next week recap) buttons; default speed 1x; remember the choice; pause/resume with the space bar.
- Rest breaks: when `weekEnded` the game pauses on a calm week recap card (from `view().recap`) with a "ต่อสัปดาห์หน้า" button (at 8x and up it auto-continues after 2 s); `termEnded` and `yearEnded` show bigger recap pages (rows, highlights, the dot picture with your room, rank and GPA movement) with the same button. Show the record line "สัปดาห์ที่ N" in the life record.
- Events and decisions: appear as a card in the story right after the block they belong to ("เกิดเรื่องขึ้น ...") with a short pause before the options, not as a sudden modal; the term-start sheet offers one big button "ใช้ชุมนุมและวิชาเลือกเดิม" next to the full pickers; the focus card keeps its place.
- Keep share cards, mock engine (update it for stepBlock, day, recap), detailed mode, themes. Verify in the browser at phone width: watch two full days at 1x, a week recap, a term end, then play through with the speed buttons; report how long one school day and one school year take at each speed.
